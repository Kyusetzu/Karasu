# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to each other. Karasu is a small project made mostly for the joy of
it, and nobody here is being paid to absorb someone's bad day.

## What that means

**Expected:**

- Assume the person on the other end is trying. Bug reports, half-finished pull
  requests and questions that turn out to be answered in the README are all fine.
- Disagree about the code. "This has a race in it" is not rudeness; "you clearly
  didn't think about this" is.
- Take a no gracefully. Some things are refused on purpose — CONTRIBUTING.md
  lists them — and a maintainer declining a feature is not an invitation to
  relitigate it across three threads.

**Not expected, and not tolerated:**

- Harassment, slurs, or personal attacks, including about someone's taste in
  anime.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwanted attention of any kind.
- Publishing anyone's private information.
- Deliberately derailing threads, or following someone across issues to keep
  arguing.
- Sustained entitlement: demanding work, deadlines, or free support from
  volunteers.

This applies everywhere the project lives — issues, pull requests, discussions,
commit messages, and the Discord server.

## Enforcement

Report anything that needs a maintainer's attention to **contact@kyusetzu.de**.
Reports stay private, including who sent them.

The maintainer will look at what happened and respond in proportion: usually a
quiet word, sometimes an edited or deleted comment with a note saying why, and
for anything serious or repeated, a ban. There is no appeals committee — this is
one person's project — but if you think a call was wrong, say so in email and it
will be read properly.

## Attribution

rewritten in plain language rather than adopted verbatim, because a policy
