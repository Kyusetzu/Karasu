Karasu keeps your AniList list in step with what you watch and read, on a phone that talks to AniList directly — there is no server in between.

On Android it carries your lists with filters, presets and bulk edits, the season calendar, statistics and a year in review, notifications, the social feed, the forum and your profile. Pull a list down to sync, hold a title for everything you can do to it, and swipe up from the bottom bar for the command palette.

Four home-screen widgets show what is airing today, what you are watching and reading, and the week ahead — drawn from the cache, with no network of their own. A background check can tell you about new episodes with the app closed.

Detection on Android is Jellyfin: point Karasu at your server and the episode playing there counts down to an update, with the screen off if you allow the tracking service to run.

Karasu fetches its own updates from GitHub releases and installs them through the system installer; it never sends anything anywhere but AniList and the Jellyfin server you configured.
